# Trend Musical Platform

## Overview
**Trend** is a free musical platform designed to promote Liberian artists by showcasing their craft, enabling monetization, and fostering direct engagement with fans. It also serves as a hub for local entertainment news and events, bridging the gap between artists and their audience.

- **Audience:** Artists & Fans  
- **Core Value:** Empower artists to earn and grow visibility while giving fans access to music, news, and events.



## Description
- Provide artists with tools to upload, monetize, and track their music.  
- Enable fans to discover, support, and engage with artists.  
- Centralize Liberian entertainment news and events in one platform.  
- Facilitate direct artist-fan communication and booking opportunities.



## Features

### Artist
1. **Song Upload** – Upload tracks in supported formats (MP3, WAV).  
2. **Monetization** – Integrated with Orange Money & MTN Mobile Money for paid downloads.  
3. **Artist Profile** – Customizable profile with bio, achievements, and discography.  
4. **Engagement Overview** – Analytics dashboard showing downloads, likes, and followers.  

### Fan
1. **Entertainment News** – Access curated local entertainment updates.  
2. **Artist Achievements** – Read about artist milestones and career highlights.  
3. **Download Music** – Purchase and download songs via mobile money.  
4. **Upcoming Events** – Event listings for concerts, shows, and artist appearances.  



## Technical Considerations
- **Authentication:** Role-based access (Artist vs Fan).  
- **Payment Integration:** Secure APIs for Orange Money & MTN Mobile Money.  
- **Storage:** Cloud-based storage for audio files and media assets.  
- **Analytics:** Real-time tracking of downloads, likes, and engagement.  
- **Scalability:** Designed to handle a growing user base and large media uploads.  



## User Flow

### Artist
1. Register  
2. Create Profile  
3. Upload Song  
4. Enable Monetization  
5. Track Engagement  

### Fan
1. Register  
2. Browse News/Events  
3. Explore Artist Profiles  
4. Purchase/Download Songs  
5. Engage with Artists
